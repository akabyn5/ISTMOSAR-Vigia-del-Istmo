# Limitations and Known Pending Work

- The map is a general OpenStreetMap basemap centered on Panama. It is not a footprint or coverage map.
- An authoritative study-area polygon has not been approved or loaded in this release.
- The three scene records contain acquisition metadata only; no NISAR raster previews are currently published.
- Scene-footprint intersection with the definitive study area has not been approved.
- Product-level QA reports, statistics, and mask interpretation require final review.
- The Change Explorer does not calculate or display quantitative changes.
- Backscatter variations can have multiple causes. They must not be labelled as flooding, water-level changes, deforestation, or another specific event without additional evidence.
- The site depends on external services for Leaflet assets, Google Fonts, and OpenStreetMap tiles. The basemap may not load when those services are unavailable.
- The current catalogue is a small project-specific set of acquisitions, not a complete NISAR archive browser.
