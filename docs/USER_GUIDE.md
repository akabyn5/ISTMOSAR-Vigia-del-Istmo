# User Guide

## Navigate the site

Use the top navigation to move between Home, Radar Explorer, Change Explorer, Science & Methodology, and About & Data Sources.

## Radar Explorer

Choose one of the three catalogued acquisitions from the scene selector. The panel displays its acquisition date, product level, cycle, relative track, frame, orbit direction, orbit-fidelity label, frequency, polarization terms, and CMR granule identifier.

The map currently shows a general Panama basemap only. No acquisition footprint or approved study-area boundary is drawn. The scene selector is a metadata catalogue, not a raster viewer in this initial release.

## Change Explorer

This section explains why quantitative change products are not yet published. It is not an active change-detection tool.

## Science & Methodology

This section summarizes SAR, NISAR L-band, GCOV, polarization, and the gamma-zero power convention. Consult the cited NASA and ASF documentation for full technical detail.

## External services

The basemap uses OpenStreetMap tiles and Leaflet. An internet connection is required for those external resources.
