04 / SCIENCE & METHODOLOGY

Radar measurements, not photographs.

A concise guide to the measurement represented by the selected product and to the checks required before interpreting change.

01

Synthetic Aperture Radar

SAR is an active microwave remote-sensing technique. The recorded return depends on the interaction of the radar signal with the observed surface and on acquisition geometry.

02

NISAR L-band

The project examines NISAR L-band observations. Longer radar wavelengths interact with vegetation and surface features differently from shorter wavelengths; interpretation still depends on surface structure, moisture, and geometry.

03

Level-2 GCOV

GCOV provides geocoded polarimetric covariance measurements as calibrated gamma-zero power, with terrain-related radiometric correction and supporting layers such as masks and number of looks.

04

Polarization and power

HHHH and HVHV are real-valued diagonal covariance terms. HHHH is co-polarized; HVHV is cross-polarized. They are distinct measurements and must not be treated as interchangeable.

RADIOMETRIC DISPLAY

Linear power to decibels

For positive gamma-zero power values, the dB representation is calculated with the power conversion shown here.

A change in backscatter is not, by itself, proof of flooding, water-level change, deforestation, or any single cause. Environmental interpretation requires contextual evidence and validated processing.

05 / ABOUT & DATA SOURCES

Built in Panama by Space Dogs.

ISTMOSAR is an ARPIP research and technology-development project within Space Dogs, a Panama-based student-led space technology group.

Project intent

Develop a lightweight public interface for exploring selected NISAR L-band observations and communicating the scientific workflow, data provenance, and limitations clearly.

Implementation approach

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript provide the static application. Leaflet supports the interactive map; OpenStreetMap supplies the basemap. Small JSON files describe the scenes and study-area status. Raster previews will be added only after processing and validation.
